## Your live feed of investor activity

The real opportunity isn't just knowing that a company raised—it's understanding the story behind the check. Which investors are doubling down on your space? Who's leading rounds versus following? Who just had a win and might be ready to deploy more capital?

<Tip>
  Top founders don't just track funding announcements—they reverse-engineer them to build a targeted, timely investor list. Activity is the best signal of intent.
</Tip>

CapitalxAI turns funding news into your **actionable investor pipeline**. We monitor and structure every recent deal into a clear snapshot: company details, funding amount, investors involved, and the date. This isn't just a list—it's your starting line.

With one click, you can take any set of investors from a recent round and begin analyzing fit, tracking patterns, and initiating outreach—all inside the same workflow where you manage your fundraise.
