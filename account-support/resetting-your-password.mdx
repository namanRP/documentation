---
title: Resetting Your Password
description: Request a reset link from the sign-in page and set a new password.
icon: key-round
---

If you can't remember your password, you don't need to contact support — request a link from the sign-in page and set a new one yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password?">
    On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?** (1), next to the Password field.

    <Frame caption="The sign-in page">
      <img src="/images/forgot-password-01.png" alt="Sign-in page with the Forgot password link highlighted" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the reset link">
    Enter the email address on your account (1) and click **Send reset link** (2).

    <Frame caption="The reset request form">
      <img src="/images/forgot-password-02.png" alt="Reset request form with the email field and Send reset link button highlighted" />
    </Frame>

    If an account exists for that address, you'll get a message confirming the link was sent. You won't be told whether the address matched an account — that's intentional, so the form can't be used to check who has one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Open the email and click the reset link. It takes you to a **Reset your password** page where you enter a new password (at least 6 characters) and confirm it, then click **Update password**. You're redirected to the sign-in page automatically once it's saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Reset links expire. If you open one that's too old, the reset page tells you it's invalid and sends you back to sign-in to request a new one — so if it's been a while since you asked for the email, request a fresh link rather than reusing an old one.
</Note>
